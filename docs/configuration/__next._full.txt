<p>Interface labels stay verbatim under <code>mirror</code> — only the prose around them follows the reader.</p>
<h2><code>knowledge.language</code></h2>
<p>The language the facts themselves are written in, which follows your code and its interface
strings rather than your readers. It is the target of the rewrite hop described in
<a href="https://github.com/MiraWision/askkira/blob/main/../retrieval/">Retrieval</a>: a question is translated into this language before it is
scored, because lexical matching cannot cross languages.</p>
<h2><code>source</code></h2>
<p><code>root</code> is where the areas&#39; <code>paths</code> are resolved from — point it at the app in a monorepo. <code>include</code>
and <code>exclude</code> are globs supporting <code>**</code>, <code>*</code> and <code>?</code>, matched against repo-relative POSIX paths.
Files listed explicitly in an area&#39;s <code>paths</code> bypass <code>include</code>.</p>
<h2><code>models</code> and <code>effort</code></h2>
<p>Both are per stage, because the stages have different economics.</p>
<p>Extraction produces a durable artifact in a batch job nobody is waiting on, so it defaults to
<code>high</code> effort — quality is what you keep. Answering happens while a person watches a cursor blink,
so it defaults to <code>medium</code>. <code>judge</code> is used by the eval loop in v0.2.</p>
<p>Effort accepts <code>low</code>, <code>medium</code>, <code>high</code>, <code>xhigh</code> and <code>max</code>.</p>
<div class="callout tint-brand"><div class="callout-label">Tip</div><div class="callout-body"><p>Sweep these against your own repository rather than trusting the defaults. Lower effort performs
better than its name suggests on current models, and extraction cost is the one number that
scales with your codebase.</p>

<h2>Credentials</h2>
<p>KIRA never stores a key. The Anthropic SDK resolves credentials itself, in order:
